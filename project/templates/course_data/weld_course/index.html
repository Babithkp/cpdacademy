Welding is a crucial part of all manufacturing, construction and innovation activities. From building towering structures to creating intricate designs, fabrication and construction offers plenty of opportunities to welders. If you are interested in making a career out of this profession and do not know where to begin, this course is a perfect fit. It provides all your answers, provides a strong foundation in welding techniques and teaches you vital safety measures used to avoid various welding hazards.

This course is a comprehensive resource on welding and its various types, practices and procedures. Divided into different modules, the lessons in this course introduce you to welding and explore welding methods. We also identify appropriate welding metals and show you how to work with steel, aluminium and other materials. We then examine different types of welding through detailed lessons on metal inert gas (MIG) Welding, tungsten inert gas (TIG) Welding and arc welding. The course also focuses on various health and safety hazards related to welding and explains how to minimise or avoid them.

This course is perfect for anybody interested in welding and who loves making things. It also meets the needs of anybody who knows a bit about welding and wants to enhance their knowledge by mastering the latest innovations in the field. The course enhances your expertise and boosts your portfolio with key marketable skills. Our approach ensures that you can grasp the welding basics and safety precautions to help you weld and stay safe. Sign up to become a proficient, confident and safe welder and acquire a valuable and versatile skill.
