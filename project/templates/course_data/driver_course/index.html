Driver Safety Awareness Training: Course Assessment

The intricacies of traffic regulations combined with the changing weather and the constant risk of accidents can create a sense of unease even for experienced drivers. This course will equip you with the knowledge and skills to become a confident and responsible driver.

First, we introduce you to the principles of safe driving, emphasising the importance of factors such as a safe driving attitude and passing a driving theory test. We explain road signals and markings to ensure safe driving and efficient traffic flow. Additionally, you will learn how to conduct pre-journey safety checks and employ specific driving tactics tailored to the unique challenges encountered while driving in the Canada.
