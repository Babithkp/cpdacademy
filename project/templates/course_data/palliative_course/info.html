All about this course

Caring for people who are terminally ill or approaching the end of their life is one of the most emotionally demanding — and most rewarding — areas of health and social care. It calls for compassion, sensitivity and a sound understanding of how to provide holistic, person-centred support to both the patient and the people who love them.

This course explains the difference between palliative and end-of-life care, how to recognise the stages of a terminal illness, and how to plan and deliver quality care that manages symptoms and preserves dignity. It then covers the practical duties that follow a death, including last offices and documentation, how to support grieving families, and the legal framework — including the Mental Capacity Act 2005 and Advance Decisions to Refuse Treatment — that governs care at the end of life.

This course is suitable for care workers, support workers, and anyone working in a hospice, care home, or home care setting who wants a solid grounding in palliative and end-of-life care practice.

In order to complete the course, you must achieve 80% or more in the final multiple choice quiz.

You will earn CPD points on completion of the course, this course will take around 1.5–3 hours to complete and the certificate will last for two years.

Units covered

  • Unit 1 – End-of-Life and Palliative Care: This unit explains what palliative and end-of-life care mean and how they differ, the types and stages of terminal illness, and how to build a holistic, person-centred care plan that manages pain and other symptoms while preserving comfort and dignity.
  • Unit 2 – Care after Death and Further Support: This unit covers the practical duties that must be carried out after a death, how to support grieving families through bereavement, and the legal aspects of end-of-life care, including mental capacity, advance decisions and death certification.
  • Unit 3 – Course assessment: A final multiple choice assessment covering everything from units 1 and 2. You must score 80% or higher to pass and receive your certificate.
