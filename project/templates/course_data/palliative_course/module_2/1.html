Introduction

A caregiver's responsibilities do not end when a person dies. This unit covers what needs to happen immediately after a death, how to support the family through their grief, and the legal duties and protections that apply to end-of-life care.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of this unit, you will be able to:

  • Discuss the duties that need to be carried out for the deceased.
  • Describe the procedures and support mechanisms used to help families deal with the loss.
  • Outline the legal aspects and laws related to end-of-life and palliative care.

Next, you will learn about Duties and Procedures After Death — the practical steps that must be followed once a person has died.
