Unit Summary

In this unit, you have covered the foundations of palliative and end-of-life care:

  • Palliative care aims to relieve suffering and improve quality of life for people with a life-limiting illness, while end-of-life care is the final phase of this, focused on the last weeks, days or hours of life.
  • Good care is holistic — it addresses a person's physical, emotional, social and spiritual needs together.
  • Terminal illness commonly progresses through stable, unstable, deteriorating and terminal phases, and recognising which phase a person is in helps carers respond appropriately.
  • Advance care planning captures a person's wishes while they are able to express them, and a good care plan is built around the individual.
  • Quality end-of-life care focuses on comfort — pain relief, nutrition and hydration on the person's terms, positioning, mouth care, and calm, honest communication.
