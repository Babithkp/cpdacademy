Farm Management and Maintenance

Acquire the right agricultural and farming skills required to manage a sustainable farm successfully and learn how to manage crops, livestock, equipment and more.

Product Description

This course shows you how to be prepared to face the challenges of modern agriculture and to perform all farm duties with confidence. We explore the fundamentals of farming and farm management. The course covers various topics: introduction to farming management, crop management, livestock management, harvesting, planting, sustainable agriculture practices, farm equipment operations and maintenance.

You will earn 15 CPD points on completion of the course, this course will take around 1.5-3 hours to complete and the certificate will last for two years.
