Learn how to run a farm in this free online course that explains how to implement sustainable agricultural management.
From cultivating crops to managing livestock, farming is a multifaceted activity. The use of modern technologies has changed traditional agricultural and land usage practices. Any aspiring farmer or farm manager thus needs a firm grasp of modern farming practices to succeed. This agriculture class provides a holistic understanding of farming and sustainable farming practices to help you become a well-rounded and successful farm manager.

From ancient methods to today's dynamic practices and innovative techniques, farming has evolved and come a long way. In this changed scenario, the need to adapt and master effective farm management has never been more crucial. This agriculture class covers all the vital components of modern farming, from managing crops and livestock to employing sustainable and organic farming practices and using the latest equipment to perform innovative techniques.

We offer a detailed curriculum that covers every essential topic required to understand and implement modern agricultural practices. We explore the fundamentals of farming and farm management. The course covers various topics: introduction to farming management, crop management, livestock management, financial planning and management, sustainable agriculture practices, farm equipment and maintenance and farm business management.
